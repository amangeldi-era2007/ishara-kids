QazSL fingerspelling reference photos (40 individual still images)

Source: Figure 1, “The Kazakh fingerspelling alphabet,” in “Single-Frame
Recognition of the Kazakh Sign Language Fingerspelling Alphabet: The Open QazSL
Dataset and a Compact Hybrid Architecture” by Nurassyl Kerimbayev, Nurbol
Beisov, and Vladimir Jotsov, Technologies 14(11), 744 (2026).
https://doi.org/10.3390/technologies14110744

Adaptation: Each file is a crop of one hand photo, enlarged to 720 pixels high
with Lanczos resampling. The original spacing and printed labels were removed.
The photos were not flipped, reconstructed or generatively edited.
Crop coordinates are in scripts/export-qazsl-photos.py.

The source figure has no photos of Ъ or Ь. A still image only shows one pose, not
the movement of a dynamic letter. These are sourced research references pending
project-specific teaching review; they are not recognition templates.

Links to original clips in the “Әліппе” playlist reference the surdo server
channel / Surdo.kz. The CC BY 4.0 credit above applies to the article photos,
not to the linked videos, which are not copied or hosted by Ishara Kids.
